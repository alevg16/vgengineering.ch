Why we build

Good ideas deserve to be built right.

It started with curiosity: taking bikes and machines apart to understand how they work, and asking how everyday things could work better. That question never left.

Today we see good ideas die because the people behind them can't reach the engineering or the expertise they need. We see products designed to be thrown away, and development so slow and impersonal that nobody owns the result. We think engineering can do better. Done right, it makes everyday life better.

  • Ideas deserve to exist.No good idea should die because its owner can't access or afford engineering. That's why we offer co-ownership.
  • Make it worth buying.A product has to earn its place: real value, a long life, or a price low enough to open new applications and reach people who couldn't afford it before.
  • Engineering is a craft.Elegant simplicity, every number justified, built and tested, honest about limits.
  • Skin in the game.We build what we believe in, and we share the risk and the upside.

Better everyday life, one well-built product at a time.

Working together

Four ways to work together.

Who we work withSMEs without an R&D teamHardware startupsInventorsEngineering teams that need capacity

Phased project

Concept, design, prototype, production. Each phase is quoted on its own, with a go/no-go before the next, so you only commit to what has earned it.

Start at any stage

Already have a concept, a CAD model or a prototype? We pick it up where it is: design, DFM, testing or industrialisation.

Ongoing partnership

Your part-time development team, on a monthly basis. For companies that need mechanical engineering regularly, but not a full-time hire.

Co-ownership

A lower fee in exchange for a royalty per unit or a share in the product. Case by case.

How it works ↓

Every project is quoted individually. A mutual NDA is available from the first conversation, and IP terms are agreed in writing before work starts.

Co-ownership

Partner, not just a supplier.

Some products are worth more than a fee. When we believe in yours, we can take part of our payment as a share of its success. We carry the risk with you, and share the upside.

Royalty per unitPreferred
A reduced fee plus a small percentage of every unit sold. Simple to set up, and it rewards a product that sells.
Equity
A reduced fee in exchange for a share of the product or the company. Suits startups with more upside than cash.
How it's decided
Case by case, after a first look at the product. Everything is agreed in writing before work starts.
